FAQ

Know before you buy.

Can I use YOMUVIA for free?

Yes. Free stays at ¥0 and includes playback, organization, A-B repeat, quick clipping, local AI subtitles, subtitle editing, the clip library, and compatible-media joining.

Will I be charged during the seven-day trial?

A payment card is required to start the trial, but your total on the start date is ¥0. Cancel before the trial ends and no monthly or annual subscription fee is charged. After the trial, the plan you selected renews automatically.

What is the difference between monthly and annual Pro?

The Pro features are the same. Monthly is ¥1,480; annual is ¥14,800 and saves ¥2,960 compared with twelve monthly payments.

What happens after 600 cloud minutes?

There is no automatic overage charge. You can continue cloud correction and translation by connecting your own supported API key through BYOK.

Where can I cancel?

Open subscription management from the Pro screen in YOMUVIA settings. You can cancel at any time and keep Pro until the current billing period ends.

Is my video uploaded to the cloud?

Playback, audio extraction, and Whisper recognition run on your PC. Cloud correction and translation do not upload the video file itself.
