Can you use it without an explanation? Are subtitles fast enough? Does clipping feel obvious? Your first five minutes can reveal what the builder cannot see.

Suggested test

  1. Download and install YOMUVIA on Windows
  2. Drop in a video and play it
  3. Turn on AI subtitles and judge the wait and accuracy
  4. Set A and B, then export a clip
  5. If possible, check Pro activation and the cancellation screen

You do not need to test everything. A short, honest first impression is useful.

Five things to notice

  • Any warning or confusion during installation
  • Whether opening the first video felt obvious
  • How long subtitles felt like they took
  • Whether clipping and joining controls made sense
  • Whether the Pro value and price felt clear

Test safely

You never need to send us your video. Do not include activation codes, API keys, or personal information in screenshots. The actions you took and the message you saw are usually enough to investigate a bug.
